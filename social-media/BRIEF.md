## What the app does (source of truth — do not invent features)
Safer.Global = personal safety app for iPhone ("coming soon on iOS", currently TestFlight pilot).
- **Watch Me**: start a smart monitoring session when you're exposed (walking home, a date, a run). Safer watches over you until you say you're safe.
- **One-tap SOS / Help**: alerts your trusted people instantly with your live location; falls back to WhatsApp + SMS so the alert always lands.
- **Live location with trusted people**: share where you are with the people you choose, only when you choose.
- **When you can't reach them, Safer does**: if you go silent / can't respond, Safer escalates to your trusted contacts for you.
- **AI check-ins / voice**: Safer can check on you and understand a voice reply.
- Audiences (site use-case pages): her (walking alone / dates), him, family (kids to school), community.
