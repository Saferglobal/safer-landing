## GRID STRATEGY

The four tiles are one **story told in posters**, kesef-style: each tile is a flat, warm poster with ONE idea, one huge navy Inter headline, and one brand doodle character standing on/near it — readable at thumbnail size with everything covered except the headline. Together they answer, in order: *what is this?* (the promise) → *when do I use it?* (walking home, the #1 use case) → *what happens if I need help?* (one-tap SOS) → *who else is it for?* (the family circle). Backgrounds alternate warm-cream and soft-blue so the row reads as a deliberate checkerboard, with the SOS tile carrying the single coral/red punch of the set. **Recommended posting order: Post 4 (Family) first, then Post 3 (SOS), then Post 2 (Watch Me), then Post 1 (the Promise) last** — Instagram places newest top-left, so the finished grid's top row reads left→right: **NEVER WALK ALONE | WALKING HOME? | ONE TAP** with Family opening row 2, and the hero promise sits in the profile's prime top-left corner. (Alternative: post 1→4 chronologically and pin Posts 1–3; pinning achieves the same top row.)

**Shared lockup (all four tiles, identical):** small **orb.png + "safer" wordmark**, bottom-left corner of the 1080×1080 safe square, same size and position on every tile — this is what makes the row read as an app's profile, not four one-off posters.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (for the render round)

On cream (#fbf9f6/#f4efe9) and soft-blue (#e7efff/#f2f6ff) backgrounds this will show as a pale box/ring. Fix per tile: composite with **screen/lighten blend**, or re-export the orb with clean transparency, before placing. Verify at 100% zoom on every background.

Compare all four tiles side by side at thumbnail size before sign-off.

## CONSTRAINTS CHECKLIST (self-audit)

- **Explains what the app does / why use it:** P1 = what it is, P2 = when you use it (Watch Me), P3 = what happens when you need help (SOS + WhatsApp/SMS fallback), P4 = who it's for beyond "her" (family location circle). All four features are real and on the site/brief.
- **One idea per tile:** yes — promise / walking-home session / one-tap alert / family circle. No tile mixes features.
- **No invented features, no fake numbers, no traction claims:** zero numbers anywhere; every claim traces to BRIEF.md or live site copy. WhatsApp+SMS fallback is from the brief. No adoption/traction implications ("families are joining" removed — captions invite, never imply usage). No rescue/police/medical promises — captions say "alerts your trusted people," never "saves you."
- **Warm, zero fear-mongering:** no dark scenes, no threats, no danger imagery; the SOS tile sits on warm cream and shows determination + the alert landing, not the emergency itself.
- **Real site lines reused:** "Never walk alone." (P1 headline, homepage H1), "When you can't reach them, Safer does." (P1 sub), "watches over you until you say you're safe" (P2), "Your whole family, one circle." + "without hovering" (P4, family-page H1).
- **Format:** all headlines ≤6 words; every headline + character + chip lives inside the center 1080×1080 of the 1080×1350 canvas; each tile works with all text covered except the headline (orb walking her home; foot on SOS with signal arcs; kid inside the drawn circle). Headline alone carries the ~110px thumbnail on every tile.
- **CTA:** every caption ends on the pilot / early-access link-in-bio; "coming soon on iOS" honored — no App Store claims. Bio link must be live before Post 1 (see ops note).
